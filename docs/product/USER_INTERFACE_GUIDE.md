# SentinelFi UI/UX Interface Guide

This guide walks through the SentinelFi platform: how to navigate, interpret, and master every feature of the Predictive Financial Intelligence suite.

---

## 1. Core Navigation and Layout

### The Global Sidebar
The sidebar is your primary command rail.

- **Dashboard** - the Home Dashboard (`/dashboard/home`) and the Executive CEO view (`/dashboard/ceo`).
- **Governance Hub** - the centralised Delegation of Authority (DOA) approval queue.
- **Project Portfolio** - all CAPEX projects, WBS structures, and project budgets.
- **Financial Intelligence** - forensics, AI insights, and real-time burn rates.
- **Expenses** - Log Expense, Project Ledger (CAPEX), and Operations Ledger (OPEX).
- **Project Financials** - Project Hub, WBS Designer, Project Budgets, and Project Analytics.
- **Corporate Operations** - OPEX Planning, P2P Procurement, Payroll Desk, Corporate Analytics, and Fiscal Setup.
- **Reporting** - Intelligence Hub, Variance Analysis, CAPEX Performance, OPEX Efficiency, and Document Archive.
- **Admin** - Command Center, Team Management, Client Infrastructure, Security Audit Log, and Landlord Support.
- **Settings** - profile, security, and notifications.

### The Tenant Switcher
Located at the top-right (user profile), this lets executives switch between business units or client schemas instantly.

---

## 2. Dashboard and CEO Intelligence

### KPIs and Predictive Metrics
- **Current Burn-Rate** - average daily spend, calculated by the forensics engine.
- **Exhaustion Date** - predicts *when* your budget will hit zero based on trend analysis.
- **Health Indicators**:
  - **Green** - sufficient funding for 90+ days.
  - **Yellow** - exhaustion predicted within 30-60 days.
  - **Red** - exhaustion predicted within <30 days.

### Narrative AI Insights
Click **Generate AI Narrative** to invoke the SentinelFi AI Agent. It analyses raw ledger data and produces a human-readable risk summary, exportable to PDF for board presentations.

---

## 3. Project Hub and WBS Manager

### Hierarchical Grid
- **Recursive Rollups** - each parent category automatically sums the budget, commitments, and actual spend of its children.
- **Adding Categories** - click the **+** icon on any row to add a sub-category.
- **Cost Centers** - every row maps to a unique Cost Center ID for accounting precision.

### Real-Time Forecasting
The **Predictive Run-Rate** widget in the project header shows the burn-rate for *that project alone*.

---

## 4. Procurement and Governance

### Requisition Pipeline (Submitter)
1. Navigate to **Corporate Operations > P2P Procurement**.
2. Click **New Requisition**.
3. Select the WBS Category / Cost Center to pull from.
4. The system blocks submissions that exceed the remaining budget (Variance Check).

### Approval Workflow (Approver)
1. Navigate to **Governance Hub**.
2. **DOA Enforcement** - you only see requisitions within your authority tier.
3. Click **Approve** to sign off. The system records your timestamp and IP for the audit trail.

---

## 5. Operations (OPEX) and Payroll

### Period Budgeting
Located under **Corporate Operations > OPEX Planning**, set Monthly or Quarterly limits for recurring costs. Use the Variance tab to see which departments are over-spending.

### Payroll Management
Manage staff base pay, allowances, and departmental allocations. Once a payroll run is confirmed, the total is automatically deducted from the relevant OPEX budget category.

---

## 6. Reporting and Exports

Nearly every table in SentinelFi supports:
- **CSV/Excel Export** - raw data manipulation in spreadsheets.
- **High-Fidelity PDF** - formal executive/audit reports.
- **Word Export** - project summaries and bid documents.

---

## 7. Settings and Resilience

- **Profile** - enable **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** for secure sign-on.
- **Security Logs** - view recent login history and active sessions.
- **Notifications** - configure variance alerts to receive real-time pings when a project enters the warning zone.
