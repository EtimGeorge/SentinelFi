# Quick Start: Your First 5 Steps

Welcome to SentinelFi! This guide takes you from a fresh login to your first predictive financial report in under 5 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Initialize Your First Project
Before you can track expenses, you need a container for them.
1. Click **Project Portfolio** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New Project** at the top-right.
3. Fill in:
   - **Project Name** - e.g., "Main Warehouse Construction"
   - **Total Budget** - e.g., `1000000`
   - **Reporting Currency** - select your base (USD / NGN / etc.)
4. Click **Create Project**.

---

## Step 2: Design the WBS (Structure)
Break that million-dollar budget into tracked categories.
1. Click **Project Financials > WBS Designer** in the sidebar.
2. Select your new project from the dropdown.
3. Click the **+** icon on the root row to add a category:
   - **Name**: "Foundation & Civil Works"
   - **Target Budget**: `200000`
4. Repeat for other categories (e.g., "Structural Works", "Finishing").

---

## Step 3: Log Your Initial Expense
Simulate spend to trigger the intelligence engine.
1. Click **Expenses > Log Expense** in the sidebar.
2. Fill in:
   - **Project**: select your construction project
   - **Category**: select "Foundation & Civil Works"
   - **Amount**: `50000`
   - **Vendor**: e.g., "Steel Corp"
3. Click **Log Expense**.

---

## Step 4: Approve the Spend (Governance)
If the amount exceeds your DOA tier, it enters the approval queue.
1. Click **Governance Hub** in the sidebar.
2. Locate your "Steel Corp" requisition in the list.
3. Review the amount and the **Variance Guardrail** (ensuring it is within WBS limits).
4. Click **Approve**.

---

## Step 5: Generate the AI Intelligence Narrative
See the predictive metrics in action.
1. Click **Reporting > Intelligence Hub** in the sidebar.
2. Locate the **Predictive Intelligence Card**.
3. Click **Generate AI Narrative**.
4. Wait 5-10 seconds for the SentinelFi AI Agent to analyse your burn-rate.
5. Click **Export PDF** to get your first professional board report.
