# Document Archive and Retrieval

The Document Archive is the "Vault" for all high-fidelity financial reports generated within your tenant. Every [Intelligence Narrative](14-REPORTING-HUB.md) or [WBS Budget Export](08-PROJECT-BUDGETING.md) is preserved for historical audit.

---

## Locating Historical Reports

### The Global Archive
1. Navigate to **Reporting > Document Archive**.
2. A list of all generated PDFs, sorted by date and report type.

### Search and Filter
- **By Date** - select a specific month or year to find historical board presentations.
- **By Category** - filter for "WBS Exports", "Forensic Summaries", or "LPO Packages".

---

## Managing Archived Files

### Downloading a Report
- **Single Download** - click the **Download (PDF)** icon on any row.
- **Bulk Download** - select multiple rows and click **Export ZIP** to grab a batch for an audit review.

### Archiving vs Deleting
- **Compliance** - you cannot delete a generated report once it is saved to the archive.
- **Obsolete Reports** - use the **Hide** toggle to remove a report from the primary view while keeping it in the underlying schema for audit purposes.

---

## Efficiency and Reliability
- **Digital Signatures** - every archived report includes a digital watermark and a **Correlation ID** from the [Audit Trail](05-AUDIT-TRAIL.md).
- **PII Scrubbing** - historical reports are automatically scrubbed of sensitive user credentials during generation.
