# Financial Intelligence Hub

The Financial Intelligence Hub is the "Deep Data" centre of SentinelFi. Where the dashboard gives highlights, the Intelligence Hub provides raw forensics and AI-driven narrative.

---

## Predictive Forensics

### Daily Burn Analysis
A heat-map or trend-line of daily liquidations. Hover over any spike to see which project or LPO caused it.

### Variance Forecasting
SentinelFi compares your "Budgeted" amount against "Projected" actuals based on the current burn. Look for the **Predicted Variance** column, a negative value means you are trending toward a budget overrun.

---

## AI Insight Generation

1. Navigate to the **AI Narrative** tab within the Intelligence Hub.
2. **Select Scope** - choose a single project or leave as "Global" for an enterprise-wide summary.
3. Click **Analyze Ecosystem** - the AI agent reads every ledger entry and commitment in the database.
4. The report identifies causes of inefficiency (e.g., "Vendor X has increased prices by 15% across three separate LPOs") and provides mitigation strategies.

---

## Distribution and Sharing

### Executive PDF Export
Click **Export Intelligence Report** to merge raw forensic charts with the human-readable AI narrative into a single, professional document.

### CSV Data Dump
Click **Export CSV** to grab the raw forensic data set for custom analysis in Excel.
