# CEO High-Fidelity Dashboard

The CEO Dashboard is the "Ultimate Executive View" - thousands of project line-items distilled into actionable, predictive KPIs.

---

## Key Performance Indicators

### Total Enterprise Burn
The average daily spend across all project portfolios. A spike signals that global liquidity is at risk.

### Projected Exhaustion Date
SentinelFi calculates the date your current cash-on-hand will be depleted if the burn-rate holds constant. If the date is within 30 days, the system highlights it in **Red**.

---

## Interactive Widgets

### Predictive Run-Rate Chart
A visual trend-line of historical spending versus future projections. If the line bends upward, the AI has detected an acceleration in procurement or payroll costs.

### Top Risk Projects
A ranking of the most "inefficient" projects where the variance between budgeted amount and actual spend is highest.

---

## Actionable Features

### Generate AI Narrative
1. Click **Generate AI Narrative** at the top of the dashboard.
2. The SentinelFi AI Agent scans all WBS categories, LPOs, and expenses.
3. A human-readable summary appears (e.g., "Project X is 12% over budget due to increased material costs from Vendor Y").

### Download Board Report
Click **Download Board Report** to get a high-fidelity PDF containing all current charts and the AI-generated narrative, ready for your next board meeting.
