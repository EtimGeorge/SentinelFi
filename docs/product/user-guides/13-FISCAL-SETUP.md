# Fiscal Setup and Categories

Fiscal Setup is where the CFO or Admin Director configures the foundational **Chart of Accounts** for the organisation's [OPEX Planning](11-OPEX-PLANNING.md).

---

## Building the Infrastructure

### Adding OPEX Categories
1. Navigate to **Corporate Operations > Fiscal Setup**.
2. Click **+ Add Category**.
3. **Fields**:
   - **Category Name** - e.g., "Cloud Infrastructure", "Office Rent", "Staff Welfare".
   - **Description** - briefly explain what kind of spend is allowed under this category.

### Defining Fiscal Periods
SentinelFi groups all operational spend into logical time-blocks (Periods). Ensure all 12 months of the fiscal year are initialised before starting [OPEX Planning](11-OPEX-PLANNING.md).

---

## Guardrails

- **Standardisation** - use consistent naming conventions (e.g., all capitals for major categories) for easier filtering.
- **Hierarchical Categories** - you can nest categories (e.g., "Administration" > "Office Supplies") similarly to the [WBS Designer](07-WBS-DESIGNER.md).
- **Deactivation** - if a category is no longer used, **Deactivate** it rather than deleting. This preserves historical data for audit purposes.

---

## Pre-Monthly Planning Checklist
1. Are all departmental categories mapped to the correct names?
2. Are all staff members assigned to an OPEX category for [Payroll](12-PAYROLL-DESK.md)?
3. Have you confirmed the reporting currency (USD / NGN)?
