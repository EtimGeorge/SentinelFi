# OPEX Planning and Departmental Budgets

**Operational Expenditure (OPEX)** planning is where you manage recurring costs for departments, rent, utilities, administrative overhead, and more.

---

## Period-Based Budgeting

Unlike projects (which are time-limited), OPEX budgets are tied to **Fiscal Periods** (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).

### Defining a Period
1. Navigate to **Corporate Operations > OPEX Planning**.
2. Select the current **Fiscal Year** and **Month** (e.g., "March 2026").

### Allocating Departmental Budgets
Budgets are allocated to specific **OPEX Categories** (e.g., "ICT Maintenance", "Staff Training"). Input the "Total Allocation" for the selected period. Every department head must ensure their spend stays within these periodic limits.

---

## Monitoring Variance

### The Variance Tab
SentinelFi compares the **Allocated** amount against **Actual Spend** from logged expenses and payroll:
- **Green** - spent < 80% of allocation.
- **Yellow** - spent > 80% of allocation.
- **Red** - spent > 100% of allocation (over budget).

### Variance Guardrails
If a department is over budget, the system blocks any new [Purchase Requisitions](10-P2P-PROCUREMENT.md) for that category until the CFO grants an override.

---

## Best Practices
- **Plan in Advance** - set OPEX allocations at the start of each quarter.
- **Review Monthly** - use the Corporate Analytics page to identify departments that consistently exceed their targets.
