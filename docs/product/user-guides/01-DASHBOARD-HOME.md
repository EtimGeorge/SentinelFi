# The Home Dashboard

The Home Dashboard is your cockpit: a real-time, high-level summary of financial health across all projects and departments.

---

## Layout
The dashboard is split into four functional zones:

1. **Metric Ribbons (Top)** - quick totalisers for **Total Revenue**, **Spent**, and **Profitability**.
2. **Health Highlights (Center)** - predictive cards showing **Daily Burn Rate** and **Risk Projects**.
3. **Active Workflow Queue** - recently created projects and requisitions that need attention.
4. **Currency Selector** - top-right toggle to switch tracking currencies (USD, NGN, GBP) and see how exchange rates affect global burn.

---

## Interpreting Risk Levels
The intelligence engine categorises every project into one of three colours:

- **Green (Stable)** - budget is healthy and burn-rate is sustainable for the foreseeable future.
- **Yellow (Warning)** - spending has accelerated; approximately 30-60 days before budget depletion.
- **Red (Critical)** - budget will be exhausted within 30 days. Immediate executive intervention required.

---

## Navigation Tips
- Click any project card in the Risk section to jump directly to its [WBS Designer](07-WBS-DESIGNER.md).
- The sidebar icons mirror the dashboard colour scheme for quick visual association.
