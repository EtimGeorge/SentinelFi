# The Payroll Desk

The Payroll Desk is where you manage staff compensation and ensure that labour costs are accurately reflected in your [OPEX Budgets](11-OPEX-PLANNING.md).

---

## Managing Staff Payments

### Adding a Payroll Entry
1. Navigate to **Corporate Operations > Payroll Desk**.
2. Click **+ New Payroll Entry**.
3. **Fields**:
   - **Staff Name/ID** - link to the internal employee record.
   - **Base Pay** - the primary salary amount.
   - **Allowances** - supplementary bonuses or benefits.
   - **Allocation** - select the OPEX Category that funds this salary (e.g., "HR Salaries").

### Confirming the Payroll Run
1. At the end of each month, select all relevant entries.
2. Review for inconsistencies (e.g., duplicate payments).
3. Click **Process Payroll Run**.
4. The total amount is immediately deducted from the **Actual** column of the target OPEX categories.

---

## Integration and Automation

### Multi-Departmental Allocation
If a staff member divides their time between two departments (e.g., a Project Manager on two projects), you can split their payroll allocation by percentage (e.g., 50% to Project A, 50% to HR Overhead).

### Burn-Rate Coordination
Large payroll runs are one of the most significant triggers for [Daily Burn-Rate](02-CEO-DASHBOARD.md) spikes.

---

## Best Practices
- **Audit Regularly** - compare payroll totals against bank statements to ensure zero leakage.
- **Set Up Templates** - use recurring payroll templates for fixed monthly salaries to avoid manual entry.
