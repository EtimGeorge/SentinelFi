# Reporting and AI Hub

The Reporting Hub is where you generate high-fidelity financial documents and leverage the SentinelFi AI Agent to create human-readable narratives of fiscal health.

---

## Generating Intelligence Reports

### The Reporting Centre
1. Navigate to **Reporting > Intelligence Hub**.
2. Choose the report type (e.g., "Executive Summary", "Variance Analysis", "CAPEX Deep-Dive").

### AI Integration
1. Click **Generate AI Narrative**.
2. The AI agent scans all ledger entries, LPOs, and WBS categories.
3. A Narrative Intelligence Card appears, explaining *why* your budget is trending in its current direction.

---

## High-Fidelity Exports

### PDF Reports (Board Ready)
Click **Export PDF** for a professionally formatted document with your company logo, current KPIs, and the full AI-generated narrative.

### Excel/CSV Data (For Analysts)
Click **Export Data (Excel)** for a granular workbook containing every transaction and budget line-item for custom external modelling.

---

## The Document Archive
Navigate to **Reporting > Document Archive**. Every PDF report you generate is automatically saved here for historical retrieval. Search by date or report title to re-download past board presentations.

---

## Best Practices
- **Consistency** - run an Intelligence Narrative at the end of every week to catch trends before they become risks.
- **Narrative Verification** - if the AI suggests a budget overrun, verify it against the [WBS Designer Actuals](07-WBS-DESIGNER.md) to pinpoint the exact category.
