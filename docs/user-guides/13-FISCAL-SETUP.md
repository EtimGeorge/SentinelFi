# ⚙️ 13: Fiscal Setup & Categories

The Fiscal Setup is where the **CFO** or **Admin Director** configures the foundational "Chart of Accounts" for the organization's **[OPEX Planning](file:///c:/temp/SentinelFi/docs/user-guides/11-OPEX-PLANNING.md)**.

---

## 🏗️ Building the Infrastructure

### 1. Adding OPEX Categories
- **Path**: Navigate to **"Corporate Operations -> Fiscal Setup"**.
- **Action**: Click **"+ Add Category"**.
- **Fields**:
  - **Category Name**: e.g., *"Cloud Infrastructure"*, *"Office Rent"*, *"Staff Welfare"*.
  - **Description**: Briefly explain what kind of spend is allowed under this category.

### 2. Defining Fiscal Periods
- **The Concept**: SentinelFi groups all operational spend into logical time-blocks (Periods).
- **The Setup**: Ensure all 12 months of the fiscal year are initialized before starting any **[OPEX Planning](file:///c:/temp/SentinelFi/docs/user-guides/11-OPEX-PLANNING.md)**.

---

## 🛡️ Best Practices & Guardrails

- **Standardization**: Use consistent naming conventions (e.g., all capitals for major categories) to make filtering easier.
- **Hierarchical Categories**: (New Feature) You can nest categories (e.g., *"Administration"* -> *"Office Supplies"*) similarly to how you build a **[WBS Designer](file:///c:/temp/SentinelFi/docs/user-guides/07-WBS-DESIGNER.md)** for projects.
- **Deactivation**: If a category is no longer used (e.g., a closed office branch), **"Deactivate"** it rather than deleting it. This preserves the historical data for audit purposes.

---

### ✅ Checklist Before Monthly Planning
1. Are all departmental categories mapped to the correct names?
2. Are all staff members assigned to an OPEX category for **[Payroll](file:///c:/temp/SentinelFi/docs/user-guides/12-PAYROLL-DESK.md)**?
3. Have you confirmed the reporting currency (USD/NGN)?
