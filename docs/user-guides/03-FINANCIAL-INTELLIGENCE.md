# 📊 03: Financial Intelligence Hub

The Financial Intelligence Hub is the "Deep Data" center of SentinelFi. While the dashboard gives you highlights, the Intelligence Hub provides the raw forensics and AI-driven narrative.

---

## 🔍 Predictive Forensics

### 1. Daily Burn Analysis
- **Visualization**: A heat-map or trend-line of your daily liquidations.
- **Investigation**: If you see a major "spike," hover over it to see which project or LPO caused it.

### 2. Variance Forecasting
- **The Concept**: SentinelFi compares your "Budgeted" amount against your "Projected" actuals (based on your current burn).
- **The Metric**: Look for the **"Predicted Variance"** column. If it's negative, you are trending toward a budget overrun.

---

## 🧠 AI Insight Generation

### 🚀 Creating a Narrative
1. **Navigate** to the **"AI Narrative"** tab within the Intelligence Hub.
2. **Select Scope**: Choose a single project or leave as "Global" for an enterprise-wide summary.
3. **Click "Analyze Ecosystem"**: SentinelFi’s AI agent will read every ledger entry and commitment in the database.

### 📝 Reading the Report
- **The "Why"**: The AI identifies causes of inefficiency (e.g., *"Vendor X has increased prices by 15% across three separate LPOs"*).
- **The "How"**: It provides mitigation strategies (e.g., *"Reducing procurement in Category B will extend liquidity for 12 more days"*).

---

## 📤 Distribution & Sharing

### 📄 Executive PDF Export
- **Step**: Click the **"Export Intelligence Report"** icon.
- **Feature**: This report merges the raw forensic charts with the human-readable AI narrative into a single, professional document.

### 📊 CSV Data Dump
- If you need to perform custom analysis in Excel, click the **"Export CSV"** button to grab the raw forensics data set.
