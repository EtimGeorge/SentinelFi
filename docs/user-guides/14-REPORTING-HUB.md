# 📊 14: Reporting & AI Hub

The Reporting Hub is where you generate high-fidelity financial documents and leverage the **SentinelFi AI Agent** to create human-readable narratives of your fiscal health.

---

## 🚀 Generating Intelligence Reports

### 1. The Reporting Center
- **Path**: Navigate to **"Reporting -> Intelligence Hub"**.
- **Action**: Choose the type of report (e.g., *"Executive Summary"*, *"Variance Analysis"*, *"CAPEX Deep-Dive"*).

### 2. The AI Integration (Predictive)
1. **The Button**: Click the **"Generate AI Narrative"** button.
2. **The Result**: The AI agent scans all ledger entries, LPOs, and WBS categories.
3. **The Outcome**: A **[Narrative Intelligence Card](file:///c:/temp/SentinelFi/docs/user-guides/02-CEO-DASHBOARD.md)** appears, explaining *why* your budget is trending in its current direction.

---

## 🍱 High-Fidelity Exports

### 1. PDF Reports (Board Ready)
- **Feature**: Click **"Export PDF"**.
- **Result**: You get a professionally formatted document with your company logo, current KPIs, and the full AI-generated narrative attached.

### 2. Excel/CSV Data (For Analysts)
- **Feature**: Click **"Export Data (Excel)"**.
- **Result**: A granular workbook containing every transaction and budget line-item for custom external modeling.

---

## 🕰️ The Document Archive

- **The Path**: Navigate to **"Reporting -> Document Archive"**.
- **The Feature**: Every PDF report you generate is automatically saved here for historical retrieval.
- **Action**: You can search by date or report title to re-download a past board presentation.

---

## 🛡️ Best Practices
- **Consistency**: Run an **"Intelligence Narrative"** at the end of every week to catch trends before they become risks.
- **Narrative Verification**: If the AI suggests a budget overrun, verify it against the **[WBS Designer Actuals](file:///c:/temp/SentinelFi/docs/user-guides/07-WBS-DESIGNER.md)** to pinpoint the exact category.
