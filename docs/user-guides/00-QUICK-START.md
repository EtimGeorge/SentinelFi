# 🏃 Quick Start: Your First 5 Steps

Welcome to SentinelFi! This guide will take you from a fresh login to your first predictive financial report in under 5 minutes.

---

## 🏗️ Step 1: Initialize Your First Project
Before you can track expenses, you need a container for them.
1.  **Click** on **"Project Portfolio"** in the sidebar.
2.  **Click** the **"New Project"** button at the top-right.
3.  **Fill the Fields**:
    - **Project Name**: e.g., "Main Warehouse Construction"
    - **Total Budget**: e.g., `1000000`
    - **Currency**: Select your primary currency (USD/NGN/etc.).
4.  **Click "Create Project"**.

---

## 🌳 Step 2: Design the WBS (Structure)
Now, break that million-dollar budget into categories.
1.  **Click** on **"Project Financials -> WBS Designer"**.
2.  **Select** your new project from the dropdown.
3.  **Click** the `+` icon on the root row to add a category:
    - **Name**: "Foundation & Civil Works"
    - **Budget**: `200000`
4.  **Repeat** for other categories.

---

## 🏹 Step 3: Log Your Initial Expense
Let's simulate spend to trigger the intelligence engine.
1.  **Click** on **"Expenses -> Log Expense"**.
2.  **Fill the Fields**:
    - **Project**: Select your construction project.
    - **Category**: Select "Foundation & Civil Works".
    - **Amount**: `50000`
    - **Vendor**: e.g., "Steel Corp"
3.  **Click "Log Expense"**.

---

## ⚖️ Step 4: Approve the Spend (Governance)
If the amount is high, it enters the approval queue.
1.  **Click** on **"Governance Hub"**.
2.  **Locate** your "Steel Corp" requisition in the list.
3.  **Review** the amount and the **Variance Guardrail** (ensuring it's within WBS limits).
4.  **Click "Approve"**.

---

## 🚀 Step 5: Generate the AI Intelligence Narrative
Now for the "magic"—see the predictive metrics in action.
1.  **Click** on **"Reporting -> Intelligence Hub"**.
2.  **Locate** the **"Predictive Intelligence Card"**.
3.  **Click "Generate AI Narrative"**.
4.  **Wait** 5-10 seconds for the **SentinelFi AI Agent** to analyze your burn-rate.
5.  **Click "Export PDF"** to get your first professional board report.
