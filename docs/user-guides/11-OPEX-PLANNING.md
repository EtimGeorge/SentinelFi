# 🏢 11: OPEX Planning & Departmental Budgets

**Operational Expenditure (OPEX)** planning is where you manage recurring costs for your organization’s departments, such as Rent, Utilities, and Administrative Overhead.

---

## 📅 Period-Based Budgeting

Unlike projects (which are time-limited), OPEX budgets are tied to **Fiscal Periods** (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).

### 1. Defining a Period
- **Path**: Navigate to **"Corporate Operations -> OPEX Planning"**.
- **Action**: Select the current **Fiscal Year** and **Month** (e.g., *"March 2026"*).

### 2. Allocating Departmental Budgets
- **Categories**: Budgets are allocated to specific **OPEX Categories** (e.g., *"ICT Maintenance"*, *"Staff Training"*).
- **The Entry**: Input the "Total Allocation" for the selected period.
- **The Goal**: Every department head must ensure their spend stays within these periodic limits.

---

## 📊 Monitoring Variance

### 1. The Variance Tab
- **The Metric**: SentinelFi compares the **"Allocated"** amount against the **"Actual Spend"** (from logged expenses and payroll).
- **The Visual**: 
  - 🟢 **Within Budget**: Spent < 80% of allocation.
  - 🟡 **Nearing Limit**: Spent > 80% of allocation.
  - 🔴 **Over Budget**: Spent > 100% of allocation.

### 2. Variance Guardrails
- **The Behavior**: If a department is over budget, the system will block any new **[Purchase Requisitions](file:///c:/temp/SentinelFi/docs/user-guides/10-P2P-PROCUREMENT.md)** for that category until the **CFO** grants an override.

---

## ✅ Best Practices
- **Plan in Advance**: Set your OPEX allocations at the start of each quarter.
- **Review Monthly**: Use the **"Corporate Analytics"** page to identify departments that consistently exceed their targets.
