# 👔 02: CEO High-Fidelity Dashboard

The CEO Dashboard is the "Ultimate Executive View." It distills thousands of project line-items into actionable, predictive KPIs.

---

## 🚀 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 1. Total Enterprise Burn
- **What it represents**: The average amount of money spent across *all* project portfolios on a daily basis.
- **Why it matters**: If this number spikes, your global liquidity is at risk.

### 2. Projected Exhaustion Date
- **The Core Metric**: SentinelFi calculates the date your current cash-on-hand will be depleted if your burn-rate stays constant.
- **Interpretation**: If the date is <30 days away, the system will highlight it in **Red**.

---

## 📈 Interactive Widgets

### 🔥 Predictive Run-Rate Chart
A visual trend-line of your historical spending versus your future projections. 
- **The "Bending" Line**: If the line starts bending upwards, the AI has detected an acceleration in procurement or payroll costs.

### 🏢 Top Risk Tenants/Projects
A ranking of the most "inefficient" projects where the variance between budgeted amount and actual spend is highest.

---

## 🧠 Actionable Features

### 📄 "Generate AI Narrative"
1. **Click** the primary button at the top of the dashboard.
2. **The Process**: The SentinelFi AI Agent scans all WBS categories, LPOs, and Expenses.
3. **The Result**: A human-readable summary pops up (e.g., *"Project X is 12% over budget due to increased material costs from Vendor Y"*).

### 📤 Executive Export
- **Step**: Click **"Download Board Report"**.
- **What you get**: A high-fidelity PDF containing all current charts and the AI-generated narrative, ready for your next board meeting.
