# 📊 01: The Home Dashboard

The Home Dashboard is your cockpit. It provides a real-time, high-level summary of your financial health across all projects and departments.

---

## 🏛️ Layout Mastery
The dashboard is split into four primary functional zones:
1.  **Metric Ribbons (Top)**: Quick totalizers for **Total Revenue**, **Spent**, and **Profitability**.
2.  **Health Highlights (Center)**: Predictive cards showing **Daily Burn Rate** and **Risk Projects**.
3.  **Active Workflow Queue**: A list of recently created projects and requisitions that need your attention.
4.  **Currency Selector**: At the top right, toggle between different tracking currencies (USD, NGN, GBP) to see how exchange rates affect your global burn.

---

## 🛡️ Interpreting "Risk Levels" (Predicitive)
The intelligence engine categories every project into one of three colors:
- 🟢 **Green (Stable)**: Budget is healthy and burn-rate is sustainable for the foreseeable future.
- 🟡 **Yellow (Warning)**: Spending has accelerated. You have approximately **30-60 days** before budget depletion.
- 🔴 **Red (Critical)**: Budget will be exhausted in **under 30 days**. Immediate executive intervention required.

---

## 💡 Pro-Tips for Navigation
- **Interaction**: Click on any project card in the "Risk" section to jump directly to its **WBS Designer**.
- **Sidebar Integration**: The dashboard icons match the colors of the sidebar for quick visual association.
