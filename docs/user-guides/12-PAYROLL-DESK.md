# 👤 12: The Payroll Desk

The Payroll Desk is where you manage staff compensations and ensure that the labor cost is accurately reflected in your **[OPEX Budgets](file:///c:/temp/SentinelFi/docs/user-guides/11-OPEX-PLANNING.md)**.

---

## 🏗️ Managing Staff Payments

### 1. Adding a Payroll Entry
- **Path**: Navigate to **"Corporate Operations -> Payroll Desk"**.
- **Action**: Click **"+ New Payroll Entry"**.
- **Fields**:
  - **Staff Name/ID**: Link to the internal employee record.
  - **Base Pay**: The primary salary amount.
  - **Allowances**: Add supplementary bonuses or benefits.
  - **Allocation**: Select the **OPEX Category** that will fund this salary (e.g., *"HR Salaries"*).

### 2. Confirming the Payroll Run
1. **The Batch**: At the end of each month, select all relevant entries.
2. **Review**: Check for any inconsistencies (e.g., duplicate payments).
3. **Action**: Click **"Process Payroll Run"**.
4. **Impact**: The total amount is immediately deducted from the **"Actual"** column of the target OPEX categories.

---

## 🛡️ Integration & Automation

### 🔄 Multi-Departmental Allocation
If a staff member divides their time between two departments (e.g., a **Project Manager** on two different projects), you can split their payroll allocation by percentage (e.g., 50% to **Project A**, 50% to **HR Overhead**).

### 🔥 Burn-Rate Coordination
Large payroll runs are one of the most significant triggers for **[Daily Burn-Rate](file:///c:/temp/SentinelFi/docs/user-guides/02-CEO-DASHBOARD.md)** spikes.

---

## ✅ Best Practices
- **Audit Regularly**: Compare your payroll totals against your **Bank Statements** to ensure zero leakage.
- **Set Up Templates**: Use recurring payroll templates for staff with fixed monthly salaries to avoid manual entry.
